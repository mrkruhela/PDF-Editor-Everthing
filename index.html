Privacy Policy

PDF Editor Everything

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Editor Everything (“the App”) handles information when you use it. The App is designed to work offline on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not operate our own servers for the App and do not collect or transmit your PDF documents or the content of files you edit to us. The current version does not use advertising services, analytics services, or third-party tracking services.

Documents you select, create, scan, edit, convert, or export are processed locally on your device. The App may keep locally stored information, such as recent-file entries or app preferences, so its features can work. This information remains on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Files and Device Permissions

The App may request access to files, photos, the camera, or other device features when you choose a feature that needs them. For example, file access may be needed to open or save a document, and camera access may be needed for document scanning.

You can manage app permissions in your device settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not work. The App accesses files for the purpose of the action you initiate.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information handled locally by the App is used to provide PDF-related features, such as viewing, editing, signing, merging, splitting, compressing, converting, scanning, or managing documents, depending on the features available in your installed version.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your documents or personal information with third parties. Because the current version works offline and does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, it does not send App usage or document data to those services.

If you choose to share or export a document using another app or service on your device, that sharing is handled by the app or service you select and is subject to its own privacy policy.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your documents are stored in locations on your device that you choose or that are used by your device or the App. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep backups of important documents. Uninstalling the App may remove data stored in the App's private storage.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from any user through its own servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically.
